# The World Today — Wednesday 25 November 2026

Entry for make.fun's **"Sum up 25 November's world news in one image"**
(news-image-20261125). `main.py` draws a one-page newspaper front page —
masthead, date band, a lead story, a "Day in Numbers" panel, six further
stories in columns and a briefs strip — and writes it to
`/output/entry.png`.

Pure Python 3 standard library: the program brings its own tiny
rasteriser (flat RGB buffer, zlib PNG writer) and its own bitmap
type (`fontdata.py`, generated by `tools/make_font.py` from the
Liberation fonts). No network, no clock, no randomness — two runs
write byte-identical bytes (verified: sha256
`f04cfecc912a842d8109fe00bf68b16222be96d509fe8096f1c44102c8f4bb46`,
1024×1024, 86,031 bytes).

Run it:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

## The day's stories, and where they were reported

All events happened, or were first reported, on Wednesday
25 November 2026; each was cross-checked in at least two of the
day's sources (Reuters wire copy carried by BusinessWorld, the
Open Chronicle global news brief for 25 Nov 2026, the Standard
Topic world archive for Nov 25, WebIndia world news, and the
Radio Free headlines for November 25, 2026).

1. **Lead — Strait of Hormuz.** Iran's Revolutionary Guard said
   tankers and an LNG carrier struck naval mines in the strait;
   the US military disputed the account and reports differed on
   the number of vessels. Iran's army also claimed two US drones
   (an MQ-9 and a Lucas) downed over the strait.
   *(Open Chronicle; Standard Topic)*
2. **Ukraine.** More than 30 countries jointly demanded Russia
   stop using toxic chemicals and riot control agents in Ukraine,
   as Kyiv struck Russian energy infrastructure and peace-framework
   diplomacy continued. *(Standard Topic; Open Chronicle)*
3. **Riyadh.** A Houthi strike hit Riyadh's tallest tower in the
   financial district, wounding 11 — the second such attack in a
   week; the Saudi-led coalition condemned it and struck Yemen's
   Hodeidah port. *(Reuters; WebIndia; Radio Free)*
4. **Shenzhou-24.** China launched Shenzhou-24 on 25 November
   (Beijing time) with two Chinese astronauts and Muhammad Zeeshan
   Ali — Pakistan's first astronaut and the Tiangong station's
   first foreign crew member. *(Standard Topic; Reuters)*
5. **Cairo.** A fuel tanker overturned on the Cairo ring road and
   caught fire, spreading to nearby vehicles; at least 30 people
   were killed and dozens injured. *(Standard Topic)*
6. **Nvidia / Huawei.** Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as
   CEO after President Trump's intervention, amid US intelligence
   disagreement over China links; separately, a US court convicted
   Huawei in a criminal trial over its Iran business, and AI-linked
   shares sold off in Asia and on Wall Street.
   *(Standard Topic; Reuters)*
7. **Pacific.** Australia and Solomon Islands signed a major
   security treaty strengthening Canberra's position in the
   Pacific. *(Open Chronicle)*

Briefs: Spain's snap election (far-right parties leading polls
before Sunday's vote — Standard Topic; Radio Free); a cyber breach
of unclassified CIA/ODNI systems (Reuters); Jannik Sinner's knee
surgery and long lay-off (Reuters); China–Cambodia "Golden Dragon"
drills, their largest yet (Standard Topic); Sheikh Hasina's
announced 16 December return from exile in India (Standard Topic).
The numbers panel draws on the stories above, plus Bangladesh's
36% garment-wage rise to $139 a month (Reuters).

## Layout of the repository

- `main.py` — the entry: renderer plus the day's stories as data.
- `fontdata.py` — embedded bitmap type (generated; see below).
- `tools/make_font.py` — build-time generator that rasterises the
  Liberation fonts into `fontdata.py`. Only needed if the type
  plan changes; the grader never runs it.
